# Escalation Policy

The agent must escalate a ticket to a human support representative when the customer asks for a manager, when the request involves legal or safety concerns, or when the agent cannot find the order or customer record needed to act. Escalation is also required for any action that the agent is not authorized to take on its own.

When escalating, the agent should write a short summary of the issue, state what has already been checked, and tell the customer that a human representative will follow up. The agent should not guess at an outcome while the ticket is with a human.
