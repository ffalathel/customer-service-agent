# Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund for an order within 30 days of delivery. The agent checks the order total before acting on any refund request. Refunds of $50 or less can be issued directly by the agent, with no further approval needed.

Refunds over $50 require human approval. The agent must escalate the refund request to a human reviewer and tell the customer that the refund is pending review. Do not promise a refund amount or timing until a human has approved it.
